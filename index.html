how to receive

byte & word

hover hex byte
scrambles into the decoded character; the matching word in the poem lights up red
hover poem word
scrambles into hex; clones of the corresponding bytes arc out from the hex panel toward the word
click byte / word
freezes it in place (dashed red outline). click again to release. frozen elements ignore every other mode
click the title
"the conduit speaks" — triggers a stanza-by-stanza retransmission; each stanza scrambles, holds, then decodes

modes — bottom toggle bar

heat zone
only bytes within ~200px of your cursor stay decoded; the rest re-scramble. like a flashlight in the dark
cipher rain
full-viewport falling glyphs. occasionally a column spells a word from the poem; very rarely, "nyxphaea" surfaces with a brighter, slower trail. on by default
drag corrupt
click + drag inside a panel to corrupt that region. each touched element heals after 1.6–3.6s. on by default

ambient

scanlines
horizontal + vertical scanlines track your cursor. they cross at the cursor with chromatic glow
cross sparkles
every ~80px of cursor travel, a radial pop spawns at the intersection with a chime
corruption blocks
random striped flicker rectangles spawn behind the page
margin ghosts
thematic words drift up the viewport edges, cycling between hex, scrambled glitch, and ASCII. the author's name has its own special look

audio

bottom-right; pulses until audio is on. the first click anywhere on the page enables audio. after that, click the ♪ button to toggle mute
